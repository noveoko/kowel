During World War I, Kovel (Kowel) was a primary logistical rail hub, field airfield center, and strategic sector headquarters for Central Powers forces on the Eastern Front, particularly during the Brusilov Offensive (1916) and the Spring Advance into Ukraine / Operation Faustschlag (1918). Both German (Luftstreitkräfte / Fliegertruppe) and Austro-Hungarian (k.u.k. Luftfahrtruppe) aviation units operated out of base facilities in and immediately surrounding Kovel.
The known aviation units documented operating in or staged through the Kovel airfield hub are detailed below:
1. German Empire (Luftstreitkräfte / Die Fliegertruppen)
German units operating at Kovel were attached to Army Group Linsingen (Heeresgruppe Linsingen) and the 4th Austro-Hungarian Army / Army Group Kovel.
Reconnaissance & Observation (Flieger-Abteilungen)
 * Flieger-Abteilung 27 (FA 27): Staged through and based at Kovel in early 1918 during the German advance eastward into Ukraine (Unternehmen Faustschlag).
 * Feldflieger-Abteilung 24 (FFA 24): Deployed to the Volhynia/Kovel sector during the defensive battles along the Stokhyd (Stochod) River in 1916.
 * Feldflieger-Abteilung 43 (FFA 43): Active in the Army Group Linsingen sector covering the Kovel rail junction (1916).
 * Flieger-Abteilung (Artillerie) 212 / FA(A) 212: Provided dedicated artillery spotter and short-range reconnaissance coverage over the Stokhyd bridgeheads east of Kovel (1916–1917).
 * Flieger-Abteilung (Artillerie) 273 s / FA(A) 273: Originally formed from Flieger Bataillon Nr. 1, assigned to heavy artillery calibration and photographic reconnaissance along the Kovel line.
Fighter Units (Jagdstaffeln)
 * Jagdstaffel 24 (Jasta 24): Temporarily stationed in the Army Group Linsingen operational area near Kovel in mid-to-late 1916 to counter Russian air superiority during the Brusilov Offensive.
 * Kampfeinsitzer-Kommando (KEK) Linsingen: A pre-Jasta composite fighter detachment formed in 1916 specifically to protect the strategic Kovel railway yards and airfields from Imperial Russian Air Service bombing raids.
Specialized Aerial Units
 * Reihenbildzug 1 (RBZ 1): Special long-range photographic survey/mosaic unit attached to headquarters in Kovel (1917–1918).
 * Armee-Flugpark 4 (AFP 4): Main supply, repair, and staging depot stationed directly at Kovel station to maintain air units on the Volhynian front.
2. Austro-Hungarian Empire (k.u.k. Luftfahrtruppe — LFT)
Austro-Hungarian air companies (Fliegerkompanien or Flik) were attached to Army Group Archduke Karl / 4th Army operating in Volhynia.
 * Flik 20 (Fliegerkompanie 20): Assigned to Heeresgruppe Linsingen in the Lutsk–Kovel sector during the Russian 1916 offensives. Later reorganized into a short-range division reconnaissance unit (Flik 20D).
 * Flik 27 (Fliegerkompanie 27): Stationed in Volhynia (Lutsk/Kovel operational area) in 1916 before transitioning into a reconnaissance unit (Flik 27D).
 * Flik 11 (Fliegerkompanie 11): Operated short- and long-range reconnaissance missions over the Stokhyd lines east of Kovel during 1915–1916.
 * Flik 14 (Fliegerkompanie 14): Operated in the central Volhynian sector with staging facilities near the Kovel rail nexus.
